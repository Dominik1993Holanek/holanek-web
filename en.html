About us

Three generations, one workshop

Vladimír Holánek founded the locksmith workshop in Patřín near Loučeň in 1991. In 2014 his son Dominik took over the business at the age of 21. Today he runs a group of four companies: metal fabrication, CNC, powder coating and plastics production.

Our story

In 35 years a small locksmith shop in Patřín near Loučeň has grown into a group of companies with two sites in Mcely.

1991

The company is founded

Vladimír Holánek Sr. opens a locksmith workshop in Patřín near Loučeň.

1995

New premises

A move into newly built premises makes room for production to grow.

2014

A new generation

21-year-old Dominik Holánek gradually takes over the management.

2017

The Mcely site

We buy a site that allows production to expand.

2020

Powder coating

Production expands with our own powder coating line.

2023

Forbes 30 Under 30 and Holánek CNC

Dominik Holánek is named one of the 30 most promising people in the Czech Republic. Holánek CNC is founded.

2024

A new laser

A Glorystar fibre laser goes into operation in Mcely.

2025

CNC machining and Spodní dvůr

CNC mills and lathes are added. The company buys a second site in Mcely, Spodní dvůr.

2026

35 years in business

Production expands to stainless steel hairpin bends and elbows. Today the group covers metal fabrication, CNC, powder coating and plastics.

Giving back

Part of our profit goes where it makes sense

Thanks to our customers' trust we're able to help. Most often animals in need, but also people and local clubs around us.

Animals

A last chance for dogs from Romania

We work with an organisation that buys dogs out of Romanian state shelters, where they face being put down. The dogs get veterinary care and travel to the Czech Republic, not to a shelter but straight to carefully vetted families. They send us photos of how the dogs are doing.

Healthcare

Dog therapy at a long-term care ward

A certified dog-therapy team with Akoušek the dog visits patients on the long-term care ward of Klaudián's Hospital in Mladá Boleslav. Before Christmas we buy presents for the patients.

Children

Volunteering days

We take our dogs to visit young children at the children's home in Mladá Boleslav and to events such as Children's Day or Health Day. The children learn how to care for a dog and practise gross and fine motor skills through games with the dogs.

Community

Local clubs in Mcely

Mcely is where we work and where we belong. That's why we support all the local clubs and associations that do something for the village.

Nature

Huslík

A wildlife rescue station and eco-centre run by ZO ČSOP Polabí in the floodplain forest near Poděbrady. They care for injured wild animals, return them to the wild and teach children how to protect nature.
